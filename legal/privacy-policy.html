Part A — Privacy Policy

1Who we are

iTHRIVE30 is published by Andrew James Liley (ABN 41 954 079 143), trading as iTHRIVE30, of PO Box 7256, Bondi Beach NSW 2026, Australia. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean that entity. "You" means the person using the app.

Rights in the programme content are held by Bondi Project (Global) — see section 20.

Contact for any privacy question, request or complaint: bondiprojectglobal@gmail.com

2The short version

iTHRIVE30 has no user accounts, no login, and no server that stores your programme. Everything you record — habits, moods, notes, reflections, goals, photos — is written to storage on your own phone and stays there. We cannot read it. We could not produce it if asked, because we never receive it.

The single exception is an optional, anonymous crash report, described in section 6, which you can switch off in Settings.

3What the app holds

Used entirely on your device:

  • Habit records — which of the daily habits you completed, on which dates
  • Details attached to habits — activity types, cups of water, fruit, book or podcast titles
  • Mood ratings — a daily 1–5 scale
  • Written entries — intentions, gratitude entries, freeform notes, challenge responses and self-acknowledgements
  • Goals and milestones — including what you wrote about why they matter
  • Step counts — read from your device's motion sensor for the current day
  • Daily photos — saved into an "iTHRIVE30" album in your own photo library
  • Progress and badges — all derived from the records above
  • Optional details you enter in Settings — first name and date of birth
  • An install ID — a random code generated on your device to identify this installation. It is not derived from you, your phone, or any account, and is not linked to your identity.

4Where it is stored

On your device, in the app's private storage, plus your own photo library for daily photos.

There is no cloud account, no synchronisation and no backup held by us. If you delete the app, change phone, or wipe the device, this data is permanently lost unless you have made your own backup using Settings → Back up my data. That backup file goes wherever you choose to send it, and its safety and privacy are then in your hands.

5What we collect

Nothing you write, record, photograph or track is transmitted to us, ever.

We do not operate a user database. We do not use advertising networks, marketing trackers, attribution SDKs, social media pixels or third-party product analytics. We do not build a profile of you, and we do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information with anyone for any purpose.

Our app stores and Sentry (section 6) may provide us with aggregate, non-identifying figures such as download counts and crash rates. These cannot be traced back to an individual.

6Crash diagnostics (optional)

If the app crashes, and only if you have left crash reporting switched on, technical details about the failure are sent to Sentry, a crash-reporting service operated by Functional Software, Inc. (a United States company). Reports are processed in Sentry's European Union region, hosted in Frankfurt, Germany — not in the United States.

What a crash report contains: the error and where in our code it happened, the app version, your device model, and the operating system version.

What it never contains: anything you have written, your habits, moods, notes, reflections, goals, photos, step counts, name or date of birth. We have configured the service so that personally identifying information is not attached to reports.

Turning it off: Settings → Privacy → Send anonymous crash reports. It takes effect immediately, and no report is sent while it is off.

Legal basis (EU/UK users): our legitimate interest in keeping the app working correctly (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You may object at any time by using the switch above.

Overseas disclosure (Australian users): crash reports are processed on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany), operated by a United States company. By leaving crash reporting switched on you consent to that overseas disclosure. This is the only circumstance in which any information from the app leaves Australia, and it contains none of your programme content.

Part B — The nature of the programme

This part is not about privacy. It sets out plainly what iTHRIVE30 is, and what it is not, so that nobody has to guess.

16A system, not a promise

iTHRIVE30 is a system. You are the operator.

The app provides a structure: a set of daily habits, a sequence of challenges, a way to record what you did, and a way to see it back. That is the whole of what it provides.

It does not do the work. It cannot do the work. Every result that comes out of it is produced by you, and is proportional to what you put in. Two people running the same 30 days will not arrive at the same place, because they are not the same person and did not do the same things.

We make no promise, guarantee, representation or warranty about any outcome. Not about health, fitness, weight, mood, sleep, relationships, productivity, income, or any other result. Nothing in the app, its content, its badges or its marketing should be read as a commitment that you will achieve a particular thing. Testimonials and examples describe individual experiences and are not typical, predictive or promised.

What the app can honestly claim is narrower and more useful: if you record what you do, you will be able to see what you did.

17Self-directed and self-recorded

You choose what to do each day and whether to do it. Nothing is enforced, nothing is verified, and nothing is ever locked — every day stays editable, permanently, including days long past.

That is a deliberate design decision, and it has a consequence worth stating: your records are exactly as accurate as you make them. The app is a mirror, not an invigilator. It will faithfully show you whatever you put into it.

Step counts come from your device's motion sensor and are approximate, as all such sensors are. Streaks, percentages and badges are calculated from your own entries.

18Not professional advice

iTHRIVE30 is not a medical device, and nothing in it is medical, psychological, psychiatric, nutritional, dietary, financial or legal advice. It is general wellbeing and personal-development content, provided for information only.

It is not a substitute for advice from a qualified professional who knows your circumstances. Do not disregard professional advice, or delay seeking it, because of something in this app.

Before you begin, particularly if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, are taking medication, have a history of disordered eating, or have any concern about alcohol use, speak to your doctor about whether this programme is appropriate for you.

The habits include physical activity, changes to diet and periods without alcohol. If you are physically dependent on alcohol, stopping suddenly can be dangerous and can require medical supervision. Please speak to your doctor before changing your intake.

19If you are struggling
