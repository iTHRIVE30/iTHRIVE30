## Part A — Privacy Policy

### 1. Who we are

Contact for any privacy question, request or complaint:
**bondiprojectglobal@gmail.com**

### 2. The short version

iTHRIVE30 has no user accounts, no login, and no server that stores your programme.
Everything you record — habits, moods, notes, reflections, goals, photos — is written to
storage on your own phone and stays there. We cannot read it. We could not produce it if
asked, because we never receive it.

The single exception is an optional, anonymous crash report, described in section 6, which
you can switch off in Settings.

### 3. What the app holds

Used entirely on your device:

- **Habit records** — which of the daily habits you completed, on which dates
- **Details attached to habits** — activity types, glasses of water, fruit, book or podcast titles
- **Mood ratings** — a daily 1–5 scale
- **Written entries** — intentions, gratitude entries, freeform notes, challenge responses and
  self-acknowledgements
- **Goals and milestones** — including what you wrote about why they matter
- **Step counts** — read from your device's motion sensor for the current day
- **Daily photos** — saved into an "iTHRIVE30" album in your own photo library
- **Progress and badges** — all derived from the records above
- **Optional details you enter in Settings** — first name and date of birth
- **An install ID** — a random code generated on your device to identify this installation.
  It is not derived from you, your phone, or any account, and is not linked to your identity.

### 4. Where it is stored

On your device, in the app's private storage, plus your own photo library for daily photos.

There is no cloud account, no synchronisation and no backup held by us. If you delete the
app, change phone, or wipe the device, this data is permanently lost unless you have made
your own backup using **Settings → Back up my data**. That backup file goes wherever you
choose to send it, and its safety and privacy are then in your hands.

### 5. What we collect

Nothing you write, record, photograph or track is transmitted to us, ever.

We do not operate a user database. We do not use advertising networks, marketing trackers,
attribution SDKs, social media pixels or third-party product analytics. We do not build a
profile of you, and we do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information with anyone
for any purpose.

Our app stores and Sentry (section 6) may provide us with aggregate, non-identifying figures
such as download counts and crash rates. These cannot be traced back to an individual.

### 6. Crash diagnostics (optional)

If the app crashes, and only if you have left crash reporting switched on, technical details
about the failure are sent to **Sentry**, a crash-reporting service operated by Functional
Software, Inc. (a United States company). **Reports are processed in Sentry's European
Union region, hosted in Frankfurt, Germany** — not in the United States.

**What a crash report contains:** the error and where in our code it happened, the app
version, your device model, and the operating system version.

**What it never contains:** anything you have written, your habits, moods, notes,
reflections, goals, photos, step counts, name or date of birth. We have configured the
service so that personally identifying information is not attached to reports.

**Turning it off:** Settings → Privacy → *Send anonymous crash reports*. It takes effect
immediately, and no report is sent while it is off.

**Legal basis (EU/UK users):** our legitimate interest in keeping the app working correctly
(Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You may object at any time by using the switch above.

**Overseas disclosure (Australian users):** crash reports are processed on servers in the
European Union (Frankfurt, Germany), operated by a United States company. By leaving crash
reporting switched on you consent to that overseas disclosure. This is the only circumstance
in which any information from the app leaves Australia, and it contains none of your
programme content.

### 7. Device permissions

The app asks for these, and each is used only for the stated purpose:

| Permission | Why | If you decline |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | To take your daily photo | You can still choose an existing photo, or skip the habit |
| Photo library | To pick a photo, and to save daily photos into an iTHRIVE30 album | The photo habit can be ticked manually |
| Motion & fitness | To read today's step count from the device sensor | Steps can be ticked manually |

None of these produce data that reaches us. You can withdraw any permission at any time in
your device settings.

### 8. Anonymity

You can use iTHRIVE30 completely anonymously. There is no sign-up, and the name and date of
birth fields in Settings are optional and stay on your device. This satisfies Australian
Privacy Principle 2 in the strongest available form: we do not know who you are.

### 9. Your controls and your rights

Because your data is on your own device, you exercise most rights directly, immediately, and
without asking us:

| What you want | How |
|---|---|
| See everything held | **Settings → Back up my data** produces a readable JSON file of every record |
| Get a portable copy | The same export — plain JSON, yours to keep or move |
| Correct something | Any day, any entry, at any time. Nothing in the app is ever locked for editing |
| Delete everything | **Settings → Delete everything**, or uninstall the app |
| Stop the one thing we receive | **Settings → Privacy → Send anonymous crash reports** |

**Australian users** have rights of access and correction under Australian Privacy
Principles 12 and 13. Since we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for
us to give you or correct — but you may still contact us and we will respond.

**EU and UK users** have rights under the GDPR to access, rectification, erasure,
restriction, portability, and objection, and to withdraw consent. For crash diagnostics —
the only data we ever receive — the switch in Settings gives immediate effect to those
rights. You may also contact us directly.

**California users** have rights under the CCPA/CPRA to know what is collected, to have it
deleted, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. **We do not sell or share personal
information, and never have.**

### 10. Data breaches

We comply with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme under Part IIIC of the *Privacy Act 1988*
(Cth). If a breach of any system we control were likely to result in serious harm, we would
notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Because your programme data never reaches our systems, a breach of our systems cannot
expose it. The corresponding risk sits with your own device and with any backup file you
have chosen to store elsewhere.

### 11. Retention

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Your data persists on your device for as long
as the app is installed, and is destroyed when you delete it or uninstall the app. Crash
reports are retained by Sentry under their standard retention period (currently 90 days) and
are then deleted.

### 12. Children, and under-18 users

iTHRIVE30 is intended for people aged **16 and over**. It is not directed at children under
16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a child. Because the app
requires no sign-up and collects nothing, there is nothing for us to hold in any event.

**If you are under 18**, we suggest going through the programme alongside a parent,
guardian or mentor — someone who knows what you are doing and can ask how it is going. The
app makes that easy without giving anything away: see section 12a. The suggestion is a
suggestion; nothing is shared unless you send it, and no feature is withheld if you don't.

### 12a. Sharing your progress with someone else

The app can produce a short progress summary and hand it to your phone's share sheet, so
you can send it to a parent, guardian, mentor, coach or friend. **We encourage this,
particularly for younger users** — someone who knows what you are working on can ask how it
is going, and being seen doing something is one of the few things that reliably helps.

It is entirely your choice, every time. Nothing is ever sent automatically, no one is
notified without you sending it, and the app never learns who you sent it to.

**A summary contains only what you did:** your Level and day, streaks, how many full days
and daily challenges you have completed, your strongest habit, and how many goals you have
set and achieved.

**It never contains what you wrote or how you felt.** Mood ratings, notes, gratitude
entries, intentions, challenge answers, self-acknowledgements and photographs are excluded
by design and cannot be shared this way. Those are the private part of the programme, and
they only work if they stay private — someone who thinks a parent might read a difficult
day will stop recording difficult days.

## Part B — The nature of the programme

This part is not about privacy. It sets out plainly what iTHRIVE30 is, and what it is not,
so that nobody has to guess.

### 16. A system, not a promise

**iTHRIVE30 is a system. You are the operator.**

The app provides a structure: a set of daily habits, a sequence of challenges, a way to
record what you did, and a way to see it back. That is the whole of what it provides.

It does not do the work. It cannot do the work. Every result that comes out of it is
produced by you, and is proportional to what you put in. Two people running the same
30 days will not arrive at the same place, because they are not the same person and did not
do the same things.

**We make no promise, guarantee, representation or warranty about any outcome.** Not about
health, fitness, weight, mood, sleep, relationships, productivity, income, or any other
result. Nothing in the app, its content, its badges or its marketing should be read as a
commitment that you will achieve a particular thing. Testimonials and examples describe
individual experiences and are not typical, predictive or promised.

What the app can honestly claim is narrower and more useful: **if you record what you do,
you will be able to see what you did.**

### 17. Self-directed and self-recorded

You choose what to do each day and whether to do it. Nothing is enforced, nothing is
verified, and nothing is ever locked — every day stays editable, permanently, including days
long past.

That is a deliberate design decision, and it has a consequence worth stating: **your records
are exactly as accurate as you make them.** The app is a mirror, not an invigilator. It will
faithfully show you whatever you put into it.

Step counts come from your device's motion sensor and are approximate, as all such sensors
are. Streaks, percentages and badges are calculated from your own entries.

### 18. Not professional advice

**iTHRIVE30 is not a medical device, and nothing in it is medical, psychological,
psychiatric, nutritional, dietary, financial or legal advice.** It is general wellbeing and
personal-development content, provided for information only.

It is not a substitute for advice from a qualified professional who knows your circumstances.
Do not disregard professional advice, or delay seeking it, because of something in this app.

**Before you begin**, particularly if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, are taking
medication, have a history of disordered eating, or have any concern about alcohol use,
speak to your doctor about whether this programme is appropriate for you.

The habits include physical activity, changes to diet and periods without alcohol.
**If you are physically dependent on alcohol, stopping suddenly can be dangerous and can
require medical supervision.** Please speak to your doctor before changing your intake.

### 19. If you are struggling

The app records mood, but it is not a mental health service and nobody is monitoring your
entries. If you are having a hard time, please reach out to a person, not an app.
